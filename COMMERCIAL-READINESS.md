# ROCKET1000 commercial readiness

South African assessment dated 13 November 2026. Scope is a paid service that analyses individual MT5 accounts, selects orders and sends them through a customer's Navigation EA to their broker. The commercial Live release is **pending regulatory, payment and operational verification**.

## Regulatory scope

- **FAIS licensing.** Sections 1 and 7 of the Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act are relevant to account-specific recommendations and automated order transmission. The service has a material likelihood of involving regulated advice or intermediary services. Automated execution under a standing customer mandate also warrants a Category II discretionary-services assessment. Have South African financial-services counsel or a qualified FAIS compliance officer determine the exact licensed entity, activities, product categories, customer jurisdictions and permitted mandate. Confirm the relevant authorisations before offering the service. Technical API access, a broker's authorisation, and customer acceptance of terms do not establish ROCKET1000's authorisation. [FAIS Act](https://www.gov.za/documents/financial-advisory-and-intermediary-services-act) · [FSCA application and licence categories](https://www.fsca.co.za/LR-FAIS-New-Applications/) · [FSCA register](https://www.fsca.co.za/FSB-Search/)
- **Automated advice and oversight.** Where applicable, section 38 of the Fit and Proper Requirements calls for competent human oversight, suitability filters, documented system design and tests, controlled model and strategy changes, and the ability to reconstruct changes. Document who can stop the engine, how a customer obtains support or review, what model and market inputs generated a decision, and how the risk checks and broker result were recorded. Have the compliance reviewer confirm disclosures, suitability, conflicts, signed mandates, complaints, record retention and any FIC obligations for the agreed operating model. [FSCA Board Notice 194 of 2017](https://www.fsca.co.za/Notices/Board%20Notice%20194%20of%202017.pdf) · [FSCA mandate circular](https://www.fsca.co.za/regulatory%20frameworks/documents%20for%20consultation/circular%204%20of%202012.pdf) · [FSCA supervisory information](https://www.fsca.co.za/Supervisory-Information/)
- **Broker and product scope.** Verify each broker's exact legal entity, jurisdiction, permission to service the intended customers, and product scope for Forex and Gold, DAX or Nasdaq CFDs. Review separately whether ROCKET1000 would itself issue, sell, market or act as principal in OTC derivatives, and obtain specialist confirmation of any applicable ODP obligations. Review actual agreements, execution and marketing flows, including any broker referral fees. Keep customer trading funds with the broker and subscription fees separate. [Financial Markets Act regulations](https://www.gov.za/documents/notices/financial-markets-act-regulations-08-feb-2018) · [FSCA CFD enforcement example](https://www.fsca.co.za/Enforcement-Matters/Lists/Enforcement%20Actions/Attachments/406/EU%20NIRVESH%20FINAL%2029%2002%202024%20%28signed%29%2815076491.1%29.pdf?web=1)
- **Privacy and subscriptions.** Document the purpose, retention and disclosure of broker-account and AI usage data. Verify operator agreements and cross-border transfers for the hosting, AI and payment providers. Assess POPIA security, breach notification and automated decision provisions. Review ZAR pricing, recurring-charge terms, cancellation, refunds and renewal notices for the actual consumer contract. Use substantiated performance claims and clearly disclose leverage, losses and outages. [POPIA, sections 19–22 and 71–72](https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/acts/2013-004.pdf) · [Consumer Protection Act, section 14](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/32186_467.pdf)
- **Commercial branding.** Verify the rights for commercial use of the existing Deadpool character, art and marks in the paid service and marketing. This review did not examine asset licences or grant permission.

## Payments and release gates

1. Obtain a written eligibility decision from Paystack for the actual South African legal entity, domestic ZAR subscriptions and the advertised automated-trading service. Paystack lists unlicensed financial services as ineligible and applies further restrictions to international payments for financial-service businesses. Sandbox access verifies integration behaviour. Live merchant acceptance depends on Paystack's review and compliance approval. [South African terms](https://paystack.com/za/terms) · [Ineligible businesses](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2127042) · [International payment eligibility](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2127682) · [Test and Live modes](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2129922)
2. Verify checkout, signed webhook delivery, idempotent replay, transaction and customer binding, cancellation, failed renewal and expiry in Paystack Test Mode. Verify provider-side currency, amount and payment reference against the app's records before granting access. Confirm the production endpoint uses the signature scheme configured in the merchant account. [Paystack subscriptions](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/subscriptions/) · [Paystack webhooks](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/webhooks/)
3. Complete private cross-device and cross-tenant acceptance, an explicitly authorised end-to-end broker Demo test, and incident and rollback rehearsals. Verify that expiry blocks paid analysis and new exposure at final authorisation while preserving broker-side stops, account visibility and authorised risk-reducing controls. Require documented legal and merchant approval before commercial activation. Preserve the independent Live kill switch and the separate approval for the authenticated Live account. Subscription status grants service access only.

This assessment records the applicable questions and release evidence. It does not verify a licence, exemption, broker status, merchant approval, compliance sign-off or commercial Live readiness.
